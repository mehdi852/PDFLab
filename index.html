Edit the text of a PDF, for real

Click any word to change it. What you see drawn on the page is a preview; on export the coordinates go to the server, which finds the original text, removes it and writes yours in its place.

  1. Click a word — it becomes editable in place.
  2. Type the replacement, press Enter. Clear the text and press Enter to delete.
  3. Or pick a tool from the toolbar: pen, highlighter, arrow, shapes, text boxes, images.
  4. Export: the server removes the old glyphs, writes the new ones on the same baseline, and redraws every markup from its coordinates.
